Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are job alerts updated automatically?

Not in this standalone HTML version. Job listings need to be maintained in the JSON file or connected to a reliable, authorised feed.

Are my files uploaded to Fine Computer?

The PDF and image tools in this page process files in your browser. The external PDF library is loaded from a CDN, but your selected files are not uploaded by this code.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your request. Wait for a reply from Fine Computer to confirm the details.

Are the listed rates official fees?

No. Shop service charges and government fees are different. Verify official charges through the relevant authorised portal.
